When people think of Orlando, they think of theme parks. But behind the scenes, the region has built one of the most interesting technology ecosystems in the country.

## A history of building worlds

Orlando has deep roots in simulation and training, digital media, and game development. Those industries share a common skill set: building immersive experiences that people believe in.

## The community matters

Over the years, events like OrlandoiX and Digital Orlando brought thousands of creators, founders and investors together. Those connections helped turn individual companies into a real ecosystem.

## Looking ahead

The next chapter will be written by the founders building here today, and by the community that supports them.
