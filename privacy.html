Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how David C. Glass, Inc. ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you visit the personal website of David C. Glass at https://davidcglass.me (the "site").

We collect only what we need to run the site, respond to you, and keep the site secure.

Who we are

This site is operated by David C. Glass, Inc., a Florida corporation. It is the personal website and portfolio of David C. Glass, and a way for people to contact him. The site offers no products or services.

The site is hosted on our own server and is made up of static pages. It has no user accounts, logins, or site database.

For privacy questions or requests, email me [at] davidcglass [dot] me or use the contact form.

What we collect

Contact form

If you write to us through the contact form at davidcglass.me/contact, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your company, if you choose to provide it
  • The topic and your message

The form also collects two technical fields used only to detect and block spam: how long you spent on the page before sending, and a hidden field that people do not fill in (a "honeypot"). We do not use these fields for any other purpose.

When you submit the form, Google Apps Script processes the submission, records it in a Google Sheet in our Google account, and sends a notification to me [at] davidcglass [dot] me through Gmail. Google acts as our processor and service provider for this.

To help confirm that your address can receive mail, we look up only the domain part of the email address (for example, gmail.com) using Google Public DNS. We also keep the sender’s email address in a temporary cache for one hour so we can limit repeat messages.

We use contact-form submissions to respond to your inquiry. We do not use them for marketing, and we do not sell them.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-GWG9E7HF43) to understand how visitors use the site. The Analytics tag is loaded from googletagmanager.com.

Google Analytics may collect:

  • Pages you view
  • The site you came from (the referrer)
  • Device, browser, and screen size
  • Approximate location (city or region, not a precise address)
  • Session details, such as when a visit starts and how it unfolds

Google Analytics 4 does not log or store full IP addresses. Our Analytics property is also linked to Google Search Console, which helps us understand how the site appears in Google Search.

We have configured Google Analytics so that Google signals and Google’s advertising features are turned off, and so that Analytics data is retained for 14 months.

You can opt out of Google Analytics at any time with the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Booking a meeting

If you use "Book a meeting," a Google Calendar appointment page opens in a pop-up. You enter your name, email address, and any booking details directly with Google. This site does not receive that information.

Google’s Privacy Policy applies to the information you give Google, and Google may set its own cookies inside the pop-up.

Server logs

Our server automatically records standard access logs, including your IP address, browser, the page requested, and the date and time. We use these logs for security and troubleshooting.

How we use your information

We use the information described above to:

  • Respond to inquiries you send us
  • Understand how the site is used, so we can maintain and improve it
  • Keep the site secure and in working order, including by detecting spam, abuse, and attacks
  • Display the site, including its typography
  • Meet our legal obligations

We do not use this information for marketing, and we do not show advertising on the site.

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we process personal information on these legal bases:

Legitimate interests. We process contact-form submissions to respond to an inquiry you chose to send, and server logs to keep the site secure and in working order.

Consent. Where the law requires consent for analytics cookies — including in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom — we use Google Analytics only with your consent. You can withdraw that consent at any time, as described below.

Cookies

This site does not set any first-party cookies of its own.

We use Google Analytics 4, which sets the following cookies:

_ga — set by Google Analytics 4 to distinguish unique visitors. Google’s default lifetime is two years.

_ga_GWG9E7HF43 — set by Google Analytics 4 to keep track of the current session. Google’s default lifetime is two years.

In the EEA and the United Kingdom, and anywhere else the law requires it, these cookies are set only after you accept them in our consent prompt. If you decline, or if you do not make a choice, Google Analytics will not load and these cookies will not be set. You can change your choice at any time using the Cookie settings link in the site footer.

You can also opt out of Google Analytics at any time with the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

If you use "Book a meeting," Google may set its own cookies inside the booking pop-up. Those cookies are set by Google, not by this site, and are covered by Google’s Privacy Policy.

Who we share information with

We share personal information only as described in this policy.

Google, as our processor and service provider. We use Google services to operate parts of the site:

  • Google Apps Script, Google Sheets, and Gmail, to receive and store contact-form submissions and notify us of them
  • Google Public DNS, to check the domain of an email address submitted through the contact form

What we don't do

  • We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising (advertising targeted to you based on your activity across other companies’ websites or apps).

We do not run advertising on the site, and we do not use advertising pixels or similar tracking from companies such as Meta or LinkedIn.

We do not offer user accounts, logins, comments, or payments.

We do not currently offer a newsletter or other marketing list.

The social icons on the site (LinkedIn, X, Instagram, and Levelge) are ordinary links, not embeds or trackers.

The site does not set first-party cookies of its own.

How long we keep information

Contact-form submissions. We keep them as long as we need them to respond and follow up, and we delete them within 24 months of our last contact with you. The short-lived rate-limit cache of a sender’s email address is kept for one hour.

Google Analytics data. Retained for 14 months.

Server access logs. Retained for up to 90 days, unless we need them longer to investigate a security incident.

When information is no longer needed for these purposes, we delete it or, where deletion is not reasonably possible, we restrict further use.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. We also use security headers and spam protection on the contact form. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold.

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you think a child under 13 has sent us personal information, please email me [at] davidcglass [dot] me and we will delete it.

Do Not Track
